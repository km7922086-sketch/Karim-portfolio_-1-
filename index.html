AI-Based Smart Irrigation System

COLLEGE TRAINING PROJECT

An automated irrigation prototype that checks water level, temperature, light intensity, and soil moisture to control irrigation and provide a low-water warning.

C#ArduinoSensorsServo Motor
Project details ＋

Overview

The system uses sensor readings and defined thresholds to decide when irrigation should run, stop, or trigger a low-water alert.

Project images

Sensors & components

  • Ultrasonic sensor to measure water-level distance
  • Temperature sensor to monitor ambient temperature
  • LDR to measure light intensity
  • Soil moisture sensor to determine whether soil is dry or moist
  • Servo motor, green and red LEDs, and buzzer for irrigation control and alerts

Operating logic

  • Irrigation starts when water-level distance is below 20 cm, temperature is above 30°C, light reading is greater than 550, and soil-moisture reading is below 500.
  • If any irrigation condition is not met, the servo returns to 0° to stop irrigation.
  • If water-level distance is 20 cm or more, the red LED turns on and the buzzer sounds to warn of low water.

My role

Developed individually using C#, Arduino, sensors, and electronic components under the supervision of department professors as part of the college summer training program.
